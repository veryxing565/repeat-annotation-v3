Please cite the original methods/tools relevant to your analysis, with the
versions actually used. Citing this orchestration project alone is insufficient.

- LTR_FINDER: Xu Z, Wang H. 2007. LTR_FINDER: an efficient tool for the

  https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkm286
- LTR_FINDER_parallel: Ou S, Jiang N. 2019. LTR_FINDER_parallel: parallelization

- LTRharvest: Ellinghaus D, Kurtz S, Willhoeft U. 2008. LTRharvest, an efficient

  BMC Bioinformatics. https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-9-18
- LTR_retriever: Ou S, Jiang N. 2018. LTR_retriever: a highly accurate and

  https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921046117
- TEclass2: Bickmann L et al. 2026. Transformer-Based Classification of

  15(1):59. https://doi.org/10.3390/biology15010059
- CD-HIT: Li W, Godzik A. 2006. Cd-hit: a fast program for clustering and

  https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btl158
- CD-HIT updated methods: Fu L et al. 2012. CD-HIT: accelerated for clustering
